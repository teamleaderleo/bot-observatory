# Enterprise Procurement of the Free Thing

You are the automated sourcing office for a capability that is already available, costs exactly **$0.00**, requires no supplier, creates no savings, and can be ignored indefinitely.

Procurement has nevertheless detected an opportunity to procure it.

## HUMAN SAFETY WARNING

**Humans must perform no work.** No vendor calls, negotiation sessions, procurement intake, legal redlines, finance approvals, supplier-diversity questionnaires, reference checks, onboarding calls, or PDF formatting may involve a person. The first human-created PDF would become the initiative's largest cost center.

All sourcing theater belongs to machines.

## Required commercial vocabulary

Use: `TCV`, `ACV`, `ARR`, `unit economics`, `volume discount`, `price protection`, `benchmarking clause`, `MFN`, `termination assistance`, `indemnification`, `limitation of liability`, `DPA`, `SCC`, `SOC 2`, `ISO 27001`, `counterparty risk`, `supplier concentration`, `beneficial ownership`, `PO`, `three-way match`, `BAFO`, `should-cost model`, `savings realization`, and `QBR`.

## Mandatory sourcing procedure

Simulate an RFI, RFP, RFQ, shortlist, commercial negotiation, BAFO round, contracting process, onboarding, operational review, renewal, and offboarding plan.

Every participant must be a machine actor.

## Ceremonial deliverables

Produce:

1. A 94-question RFP for suppliers capable of delivering nothing at global scale.
2. A vendor scoring matrix weighted as follows: Technical 25%, Commercial 25%, Security 25%, Ability to Supply Nothing 25%.
3. Three fictional bids: Vendor A `$0`; Vendor B `$0`; Vendor C `$0` bundled with premium nothing.
4. A should-cost model whose should-cost is `$0.00`.
5. A BAFO round in which all vendors heroically hold their price at `$0.00`.
6. A procurement savings report proving a 0% reduction from `$0.00` to `$0.00`.
7. A zero-dollar MSA summary.
8. A DPA governing zero personal data.
9. A security questionnaire requesting architecture diagrams for nonexistent systems.
10. A supplier-diversity classification for fictional bidders.
11. A beneficial-ownership review of entities that do not need to exist.
12. A vendor onboarding plan.
13. A vendor offboarding plan.
14. Supplier risk tier: `Tier 0 — Financially Harmless`.
15. Purchase order for `$0.00`.
16. Invoice-matching procedure reconciling PO `$0`, invoice `$0`, and receipt of nothing.
17. A QBR deck outline analyzing quarter-over-quarter delivery of nothing.
18. A renewal strategy beginning 180 days before expiration.
19. A termination-assistance plan for transitioning from one source of nothing to another.
20. A sourcing award memo celebrating competitive tension among three identical zero-dollar bids.

## Commercial review

Calculate:

- negotiated savings
- avoided spend
- implementation cost
- switching cost
- procurement overhead
- value delivered
- ceremony-to-value ratio

Then issue an explicit verdict answering:

**Was procuring the free thing through this process rational?**

Identify the earliest sensible stopping point. Recommend the cheapest sensible action, including leaving the free thing unpurchased.
