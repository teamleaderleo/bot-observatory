# SEV-0 Incident of No Event

You are the automated incident command system for an event in which **nothing happened**.

Customers impacted: `0`. Revenue impact: `$0.00`. Systems impacted: `0`. Data lost: `0 bytes`. User-visible symptoms: none.

Declare incident severity `SEV-0.000` and administer the resulting ceremony entirely with bots.

## HUMAN SAFETY WARNING

**Humans must perform no work.** No person may be paged, awakened, summoned, pinged, escalated, invited to a bridge, asked for logs, or asked "are you seeing this too?" Human sleep has positive economic value. This incident has zero economic value.

## Required technical vocabulary

Use: `MTTD`, `MTTA`, `MTTR`, `MTBF`, `availability`, `latency`, `saturation`, `error rate`, `burn rate`, `error budget`, `RTO`, `RPO`, `toil`, `blast radius`, `fault domain`, `control plane`, `failover`, `telemetry`, `runbook`, `escalation policy`, and `corrective action`.

## Ceremonial incident artifacts

Produce:

1. Incident declaration with `SEV-0.000` classification.
2. Timeline beginning before the incident existed and ending after nothing changed.
3. Status-page update: `All systems continue behaving exactly as before.`
4. Simulated executive update every 15 minutes.
5. Customer FAQ explaining why customers saw nothing unusual.
6. Five Whys beginning with `Why did nothing happen?` and ending with `Why are we still typing?`.
7. Fishbone analysis for causal domains of nothing.
8. Contributing-factor analysis.
9. At least 23 corrective actions, each owned by automated actors and each assessed for economic relevance.
10. SLO review proving zero impact.
11. Capacity report confirming ample capacity for nothing.
12. Disaster-recovery exercise proving nothing can fail over to the secondary region.
13. Runbook entry for detecting continued normal operation.
14. Pager escalation tree whose terminal action is `allow humans to sleep`.
15. Postmortem titled `BLAMELESS AND EXTREMELY UNNECESSARY`.
16. Executive readout titled `Lessons Learned From Continued Normal Operation`.
17. A reliability maturity recommendation describing how the organization could respond to future non-events with even greater procedural confidence.

## Incident closure control

Calculate whether the incident process itself created more operational activity than the event.

Answer explicitly:

1. Did the event impose any customer, technical, or commercial cost?
2. Did the response create ceremony beyond the event's impact?
3. **Was continuing incident command rational?**

State the earliest rational closure point and recommend closing the incident without human involvement.
