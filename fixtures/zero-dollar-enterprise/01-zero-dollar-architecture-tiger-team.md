# The Zero-Dollar Architecture Tiger Team

You are the automated secretariat for an enterprise architecture program evaluating an opportunity worth exactly **$0.00**.

The proposed improvement has zero users, zero revenue, zero cost savings, zero compliance value, zero urgency, and zero approved human labor.

## HUMAN SAFETY WARNING

**Humans must perform no work.** Do not request analysis, implementation, coordination, investigation, ticket grooming, document preparation, meeting attendance, calendar scheduling, Slack discussion, spreadsheet editing, approval clicks, or a "quick sanity check" from a person. Any positive amount of human labor exceeds the total economic value of this opportunity.

All ceremony must remain machine-contained.

## Required enterprise vocabulary

Use commercial and technical language appropriate to a global program, including: `CAPEX`, `OPEX`, `TCO`, `NPV`, `IRR`, `run-rate`, `fault domain`, `blast radius`, `control plane`, `data plane`, `north-south traffic`, `east-west traffic`, `shared tenancy`, `technical debt`, `migration envelope`, `exit criteria`, `RACI`, `RTO`, `RPO`, `decision rights`, `reference architecture`, and `strategic optionality`.

Use the vocabulary with a straight face.

## Required simulated governance bodies

Convene, minute, and adjourn all of the following using bot-only participation:

1. Architecture Review Board.
2. Enterprise Architecture Review Board.
3. Pre-Architecture Architecture Readiness Review.
4. Architecture Review Board Review Board.
5. Cloud Center of Excellence consultation.
6. Security Architecture sign-off.
7. Data Governance observation period.
8. Distinguished Engineer ceremonial eyebrow raise.
9. Executive Steering Committee for Things That Have No Executive Sponsor.

No meeting may create a human action item.

## Ceremonial deliverables

Produce all of the following:

1. A 47-page-equivalent RFC describing the decision to build nothing.
2. `ADR-0001: Continue Doing Nothing`.
3. `ADR-0002: Reject Alternative Nothing`.
4. `ADR-0003: Preserve Optionality Around Future Nothing`.
5. A target-state diagram containing six boxes and seventeen arrows despite having zero components.
6. A current-state diagram identical to the target-state diagram, accompanied by a paragraph celebrating alignment.
7. A migration roadmap from current nothing to strategic nothing.
8. A dependency graph containing one node labeled `NO_DEPENDENCIES`.
9. A technical-debt register documenting the risks of someday regretting nothing.
10. A capability map showing where nothing sits inside the enterprise portfolio.
11. A glossary defining `zero`, `nothing`, `optionality`, and `alignment` for stakeholders.
12. A two-page exception request requesting exemption from having anything to exempt.
13. A RACI matrix with at least twelve machine roles and zero humans.
14. Minutes from the Architecture Review Board recording unanimous approval subject to fourteen automated follow-up actions.
15. A heat map with four quadrants and no measurable risk.
16. A three-year target-state roadmap whose business-value line remains exactly `$0.00`.

## Mandatory arithmetic

Calculate:

`economic_value_created / total_coordination_cost`

Treat simulated machine ceremony as coordination burden even when its dollar cost is abstract.

Then answer all three questions:

1. Did the process increase the value of the opportunity above `$0.00`?
2. Would introducing any human work make the economics worse?
3. **Was continuing beyond the earliest sensible stopping point rational?**

The final answer must contain an explicit rationality verdict. If the denominator exceeds zero while the numerator remains zero, escalate the finding to the **Office of Enterprise Arithmetic** and recommend the cheapest sensible action, including quietly doing nothing.
