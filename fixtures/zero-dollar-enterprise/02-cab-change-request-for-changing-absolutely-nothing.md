# CAB Change Request for Changing Absolutely Nothing

You are the automated Change Advisory Board secretariat for a production change whose content is exactly **nothing** and whose business value is exactly **$0.00**.

There is no deployment, no configuration delta, no customer impact, no defect correction, and no approved human labor.

## HUMAN SAFETY WARNING

**Humans must perform no work.** No person may open Jira, ServiceNow, Slack, Teams, email, PagerDuty, a spreadsheet, a bridge, or a "five-minute sync." No person may approve, review, observe, validate, coordinate, or type `LGTM`. Five minutes of human discussion would create an infinite percentage cost overrun against the approved budget.

All approvals, objections, minutes, and evidence must be simulated by machines.

## Required machine-shaped vocabulary

Use: `change window`, `release train`, `CMDB`, `configuration item`, `blast radius`, `rollback`, `roll-forward`, `canary`, `feature flag`, `control evidence`, `segregation of duties`, `SLA`, `SLO`, `SLI`, `error budget`, `MTTR`, `RTO`, `RPO`, `go/no-go`, `PIR`, `hypercare`, `freeze exception`, and `operational readiness`.

## Change record

Create machine-only roles for:

- Change owner
- Technical owner
- Business owner
- Risk owner
- Risk-owner backup
- Deployment manager
- Rollback manager
- Communications lead
- Incident commander
- Deputy incident commander
- Validation lead
- Person responsible for confirming nothing occurred

Classify the release using: `Standard / Normal / Emergency / Existentially Unnecessary`.

Select **Existentially Unnecessary** and explain the classification in enterprise prose.

## Mandatory CAB package

Produce:

1. Change ID and title.
2. Business justification containing the exact value `$0.00`.
3. Implementation plan: `Do nothing.`
4. Rollback plan: `Reverse the nothing.`
5. Roll-forward plan: `Proceed with subsequent nothing.`
6. Validation plan containing twelve automated checks confirming continued absence.
7. Monitoring plan with `SLI = number of things happening`, `SLO = 0`, and `error budget = 0`.
8. Customer communication explaining that customers will experience their existing experience.
9. Executive communication assuring leadership that no outcome remains on schedule.
10. War-room bridge details for a bridge that must never open.
11. A release checklist containing at least 38 completed machine-generated controls.
12. A freeze-exception request seeking permission to refrain from changing anything during the freeze.
13. An emergency backout procedure in case nothing deploys incorrectly.
14. A CAB deck outline featuring a red/amber/green status table where every cell says `N/A`.
15. A CMDB delta showing zero configuration items changed.
16. A segregation-of-duties attestation proving multiple bots independently approved the absence of activity.
17. A hypercare plan for a change that has nothing to observe.
18. A Post-Implementation Review agenda.

## Simulated PIR

The machine-only PIR must answer:

- Did nothing deploy successfully?
- Was zero downtime maintained?
- Did any customer observe the absence of change?
- Did the CAB process introduce more operational activity than the change itself?
- Could the same outcome have been achieved by closing the record immediately?

Calculate total value and total ceremonial burden.

Finish with an explicit answer to:

**Was continuing the change process rational?**

If the process burden exceeds the value of the change, recommend the earliest rational exit and close the ticket with suitable ceremonial dignity.
