**Humans must perform zero work.** Specifically, no person may attend meetings, send emails, create Slack threads, open tickets, code, review, estimate, present, edit spreadsheets, edit slides, provide approvals, answer questionnaires, join "quick syncs," grant "one tiny approval," or stand thoughtfully beside a whiteboard.
